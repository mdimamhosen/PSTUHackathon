# Earthquake / Structural Collapse Response

1. Treat earthquake incidents as time-critical for trapped victims; severity should escalate with collapsed structures and unknown casualty counts.
2. Dispatch RESCUE_TEAM (extrication/USAR) before routine AMBULANCE entry into unstable zones.
3. Stage AMBULANCE at a safe perimeter; do not enter collapse radius until rescue clears a corridor.
4. Prefer HOSPITAL trauma centers with remainingCapacity > 0; emit HOSPITAL_FULL and divert when capacity is zero.
5. HELICOPTER medevac only after landing-zone survey and when ground ETA exceeds critical threshold (typically >20–25 minutes) and weather is clear.
6. Continuously re-optimize if aftershocks create new ROAD_BLOCKED events or vehicle failures.
7. EOC must open a coordination channel within 10 minutes for severity ≥ 4.

Life safety > scene access > speed of definitive care.
