# Bangladesh Multi-Region EOC Coordination Playbook

1. Regions operate semi-autonomously (Dhaka, Chattogram, Khulna, Sylhet) with national EOC oversight for cross-region surge.
2. Cross-region helicopter or specialty team transfer requires EOC approval when local capacity is exhausted.
3. Coastal cyclone and flood seasons: pre-stage RESCUE_TEAM and AMBULANCE outside projected inundation, then push inward after passage.
4. Monsoon ROAD_BLOCKED events are common — prefer Dijkstra/mesh detours or alternate resource bases rather than forcing blocked corridors.
5. Notify regional EOC via Telegram/email on severity ≥ 4 assignments and on cascading reoptimization after multiple failures.
6. Maintain explainable audit trail: score/cost, ETA source, SOP citations for after-action review.
