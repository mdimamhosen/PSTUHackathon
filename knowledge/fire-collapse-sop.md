# Fire and Building Collapse SOP

1. Dispatch RESCUE_TEAM specialized in extrication first.
2. AMBULANCE staging must remain outside collapse zone radius.
3. HOSPITAL burn/trauma capacity is preferred over nearest general bed when severity ≥ 4.
4. HELICOPTER medevac when ground ETA exceeds 25 minutes and weather is clear.
5. Continuously re-optimize if vehicle failure or hospital full events occur.
