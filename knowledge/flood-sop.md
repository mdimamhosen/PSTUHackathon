# Flood Emergency SOP

When coastal or riverine flooding is reported, prioritize water-rescue capable teams and elevated hospital routing.

1. Confirm inundation zone and isolate blocked roads.
2. Dispatch RESCUE_TEAM with boats before standard ambulances when water depth exceeds safe vehicle clearance.
3. Use HELICOPTER only when weather allows and landing zones are clear.
4. Pre-alert HOSPITAL surge capacity; divert if remainingCapacity is zero.
5. Establish EOC coordination channel within 10 minutes of severity ≥ 4.

Citation priority: life safety, then accessibility, then speed.
