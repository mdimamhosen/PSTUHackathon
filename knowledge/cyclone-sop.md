# Cyclone / Coastal Storm Protocol

Cyclone response emphasizes early evacuation logistics and post-landfall trauma care.

1. Before landfall, stage AMBULANCE and RESCUE_TEAM outside projected cone.
2. Suspend HELICOPTER operations during storm-force winds.
3. After passage, clear ROAD_BLOCKED events before ETA-critical routing.
4. Prioritize incidents with high timeSensitivity and large affectedCount.
5. Hospitals in surge mode should reject non-critical transfers.
